Gap recurrence

Polignac and Twin Primes

This track treats fixed even prime gaps as chamber words whose infinite recurrence must be described by PGS structure.

status: proof-directed probe track claim status: unresolved latest implementation: lag-2 exposure probe

Original Problem

Twin primes ask whether the gap 2 occurs infinitely often between consecutive primes. Polignac's conjecture asks whether every positive even gap occurs infinitely often between consecutive primes.

PGS Translation

Object

A fixed even gap is an ordered chamber width with a corresponding divisor-count interior and endpoint grammar.

Recurrence Form

The target becomes infinite recurrence of allowed PGS chamber words, with twin primes as the minimal nonzero even-width case.
