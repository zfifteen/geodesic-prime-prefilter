Initial Problem Tracks

Square-chamber occupancy

Legendre's Conjecture

Asks whether every interval between consecutive integer squares contains a prime. The PGS translation focuses on forcing next-prime endpoint arrival before the next square boundary.

Prime-square chamber occupancy

Brocard's Conjecture

Asks whether there are at least four primes between consecutive prime squares. The PGS translation treats prime-square chambers as ordered endpoint domains.

Difference grammar

Gilbreath's Conjecture

Asks about repeated absolute differences of the prime sequence. The PGS translation studies whether ordered gap grammar preserves the leading difference condition.

Gap recurrence

Polignac and Twin Primes

Asks about infinite recurrence of fixed even prime gaps. The PGS translation treats gap values as chamber words whose recurrence must be explained structurally.

Divisor-field extremals

Divisor-Field Extremals

Covers divisor-count and divisor-sum problems where the divisor field is the primary object rather than a side calculation.

Public endpoint closure

Endpoint Determinacy

Covers factorization-adjacent public modulus problems through endpoint-class determinacy, not through classical candidate-factor search.
