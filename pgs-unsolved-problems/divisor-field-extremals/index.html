Divisor-field extremals

Divisor-Field Extremals

This track collects problems where divisor-count, divisor-sum, and prime-factor fields are central mathematical objects.

status: research-design pending claim status: unresolved implementation: none

Original Problem Family

This track covers divisor-field problems such as Dirichlet's divisor problem, odd perfect numbers, quasiperfect numbers, weird numbers, amicable pairs, Giuga numbers, Carmichael-number growth, and related tau, sigma, omega, and phi questions.

PGS Translation

Object

The divisor field is not auxiliary in PGS. It is the measured structure that defines prime detection, chamber interiors, GWR selection, and composite load.

Extremal Form

The target becomes a study of how local divisor-count and divisor-sum structure accumulates into global extremal behavior.
