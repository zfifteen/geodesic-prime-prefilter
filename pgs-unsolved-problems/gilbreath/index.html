Difference grammar

Gilbreath's Conjecture

This track asks whether PGS gap grammar can explain the stability of the leading value under repeated absolute differences of the prime sequence.

status: proof-directed measurement active claim status: unresolved implementation: deterministic probes

Original Problem

Start with the sequence of primes. Repeatedly replace each row by the absolute differences of consecutive entries. Gilbreath's conjecture states that the first entry of every later row is 1.

PGS Translation

Object

The first difference row is the ordered prime-gap sequence. Later rows are repeated absolute-difference reductions of that gap grammar.

Grammar Form

The target becomes a stability law for the leading edge of the PGS gap-word cascade.

Research Card

First goalProve the seeded ordered prime-gap absolute-difference cascade preserves leading value 1 at every depth.
Current PGS assetsOrdered prime-gap state, gap-type research, GWR-selected chamber markers, and existing twin/gap research surfaces.
Missing bridge theoremDerive prefix admission for every depth from actual PGS ordered endpoint-gap grammar. The current target is reserve separation: high low-prefix boundary pairs such as 2,6 may occur, but they must stay away from the floor where a reset is needed.
Deterministic probe designBuild finite gap-word cascades from exact PGS-generated prime endpoints, test boundary reserve at the low-prefix exit, test the right-edge fold-transfer guard and actual ceiling margin, and enumerate abstract admitted low-prefix words that the actual PGS grammar must exclude.
Falsification conditionA proposed grammar law fails if an actual seeded endpoint-gap prefix violates transfer-guard admission, actual ceiling admission, reset admission, stop-wall behavior, or leading-edge preservation.
Statusunresolved. The measured actual endpoint surface has no transfer-guard or ceiling failure; absolute 2,6 exclusion is invalidated, and the live target is reserve separation.

Latest Advance

Actual PGS contains the boundary pair 2,6, so excluding every 2,6 boundary is false. The 2026-05-23 boundary-reserve probe found 6 measured 2,6 low-prefix exits, with minimum reserve ell = 22.

The dangerous floor case did not occur: across 19,996 cascade rows from 20,000 exact seeded endpoint gaps through final endpoint 224,743, the probe found 0 hits of 1,2,2,6,2, 1 floor row with boundary pair 2,4, 0 high-boundary floor hits, and 0 depletion failures. The next theorem obligation is reserve separation: derive why high boundary pairs stay away from the floor from ordered endpoint-gap grammar, GWR-selected chamber state, or DNI chamber structure.
