PGS Objects

Post-First Tail

For a prime root a, let u1,u2,u3,u4 be the first four endpoint offsets after a^2. The post-first endpoint-chain tail is:

c2 = u2 - u1
c3 = u3 - u2
c4 = u4 - u3

Dominant And Residual Parts

Let M be the largest of the three tail components. Let R be the sum of the other two tail components.

M = max(c2,c3,c4)
R = c2 + c3 + c4 - M

Conditional Bridge Lemma

The live high-root chain-tail target is c2 + c3 + c4 < a/2. The aggregate compensation split gives a direct arithmetic bridge:

If M < a/3 and R < a/6,
then c2 + c3 + c4 = M + R < a/3 + a/6 = a/2.

This is now the proof-facing shape. The next mathematical task is not to restore the false equal split. It is to derive dominant-tail third control and residual-pair sixth control from PGS endpoint-chain structure.

Proof-State Change

The Brocard high-root tail proof is no longer a vague aggregate-budget target. It now has two named PGS endpoint-chain obligations:

Dominant-tail third lemma:
  M = max(c2,c3,c4) < a/3.

Residual-pair sixth lemma:
  R = c2 + c3 + c4 - M < a/6.

Together, these imply the chain-tail half lemma. Combined with the square-tail quarter bridge u1 < a/4, they imply u4 < 3a/4, and therefore the prior half-wall bridge u4 < 2a for high roots.
