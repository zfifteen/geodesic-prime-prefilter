Proof Obligation

The next theorem target is the half-wall endpoint lemma:

For every prime root a >= 11, the fourth exact-divisor-count endpoint after a^2 satisfies e4(a) - a^2 < 2a.

That lemma implies the Brocard chamber bridge for all consecutive odd primes a < b with a >= 11, because b >= a + 2, hence a^2 + 2a < (a + 2)^2 <= b^2. The roots 3, 5, 7 remain finite direct rows under the original 4a wall.
