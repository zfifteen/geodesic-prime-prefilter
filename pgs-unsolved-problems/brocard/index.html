Prime-square chamber occupancy

Brocard's Conjecture

This track asks whether PGS can account for repeated prime occupancy inside the chamber between consecutive prime squares.

status: research-design pending claim status: unresolved implementation: none

Original Problem

For consecutive primes p_k and p_(k+1), there are at least four primes between p_k^2 and p_(k+1)^2.

PGS Translation

Object

The interval (p_k^2, p_(k+1)^2) is a prime-square chamber whose width is determined by adjacent prime endpoints.

Occupancy Form

The target becomes a lower bound on endpoint arrivals inside a chamber whose walls are themselves generated by the ordered prime-gap state.

Research Card

First goalDefine the end goal and research design for a PGS account of four-prime occupancy between consecutive prime-square boundaries.
Current PGS assetsSquare-branch characterization, prime-square envelope notes, GWR selected-integer theorem, gap-type material, and square-ruler evidence surfaces.
Missing bridge theoremA theorem that turns PGS prime-square chamber structure into a uniform lower bound on the number of prime endpoints inside the chamber.
Deterministic probe designFor each consecutive prime pair, compute chamber width, internal endpoint count, GWR witnesses for each internal gap, and square-ruler state.
Falsification conditionA proposed bridge fails if the PGS chamber conditions do not force four endpoint arrivals or if measured chamber classes split into unresolved families not covered by the bridge.
