## Debate Summary, Convergences, and Selection Rationale

**Evaluation dimensions used (agreed across agents):**
- Direct advancement of proved theorems vs. conversion of measured/empirical surfaces into theorems.
- Removal of the highest-leverage current blockers (square-branch D(r) obligation; 50-bit and future unresolved RSA cases; unnamed chamber/chain objects; scaling wall at cryptographic sizes).
- Force-multiplier effect across tracks (cryptology data pressures bounded compression and state budget; certificates enable Lean; large natural gaps from moduli feed gap-type and falsification work).
- Strict adherence to PGS-first entry point and generator contract.
- External credibility / citable impact without drift.
- Feasibility on a 1–3 year horizon with existing momentum.

**Strong convergences:**
- The program is past the "build a better generator" phase. v1.1 is a completed milestone.
- Purely incremental measurement without new invariants or forcing applications is low leverage.
- Any winning upgrade must generate new classes of artifacts (structural certificates, public endpoint classes at real bit lengths, machine-checkable lemmas, or decisive counterexample catalogs) that feed other frontiers.
- Shape violations (classical first-frame, downgrading PROOF.md theorems, treating generator as whole program) were actively policed by the Continuity Keeper and rejected.

**Key dissent and pressure:**
- Bounded Compression Lead argued that declaring any large-scale result while the square-branch obligation remains open builds on an empirical foundation that could still crack.
- Theorem Guardian and Formalist argued that without machine-checked core, external mathematicians will discount everything as "empirical up to 10^18."
- Cryptologic Strategist + High-Scale + State Budget countered that only work that produces publicly checkable results on real cryptographic moduli will generate the sustained pressure, data, and external scrutiny needed to close the other hard problems (including square branch via the gaps inside semiprimes).
- Continuity Keeper vetoed treating high-scale C productionization or broad predictions as the flagship before the underlying chain-horizon / endpoint-determinacy / chamber objects are named and carded.

**Decision rule applied:**
Select the single upgrade that (1) most powerfully amplifies the deterministic PGS core across the largest number of active frontiers, (2) directly attacks a visible high-stakes unresolved application surface (RSA endpoints) while generating exactly the data needed for the current central obligation (square branch), (3) produces new public, auditable artifacts whose correctness can be confirmed classically after the fact without the audit defining the inference, and (4) can be executed while rigorously maintaining PGS-native inference and state separation.

**Winner:** Maturation and generalization of the deterministic PGS-native RSA modulus endpoint resolver — specifically, adopting explicit GWR-selected carrier transport closure (under reciprocal floor maps on chamber-reset certificates) together with structural certificates, to the point where the program can routinely resolve (or cleanly emit unresolved for) endpoint classes on cryptographically relevant (256-bit to 1024-bit+) moduli.

This is the proposal family most strongly advanced by the Cryptologic Strategist, reinforced as forcing function by State Budget Analyst and High-Scale Engineer, and accepted by the majority after pressure testing as the highest single-leverage move for the whole program.

## Lengthy Explanation of the Winner

**Winner (precise statement):** Complete the transition from rung-specific measured demonstrations of RSA endpoint structure to a general, deterministic, PGS-native engine that, given an RSA modulus (or family of moduli), traverses locked endpoint chains using only PGS objects and rules (GWR-selected carriers inside chamber-reset certificates, floor transport, reciprocal endpoint closure conditions, modulus-link residual classification), emits a public structural certificate when the invariants close to an oriented endpoint class, and emits an explicit, diagnosable "unresolved" state (with residual) when they do not — all while scaling reliably to 256-bit, 512-bit, and 1024-bit+ examples and remaining strictly inside the generator contract and AGENTS.md cryptology contract.

### PGS Objects and Invariants (entry point, per every agent and contract)
Start at a known anchor or previous public endpoint. Build the ordered prime-gap state and divisor-count field in the local chamber. Apply the GWR / leftmost minimum-divisor rule (proved in PROOF.md) to identify the selected carrier (often d=4 under square exclusion on current surfaces). Record the chamber-reset certificate containing the carrier fields (carrier_w / carrier_d, lock_carrier, threat, reset_signature, etc.). Choose the oriented transport coordinate relative to floor(sqrt(N)). Perform floor transport floor(N / carrier). Test reciprocal closure conditions (deadline-signature correction, mutual certificate images, explicit GWR-carrier alignment invariants). On success emit the public endpoint class plus structural certificate. On failure emit the modulus-link residual with full diagnostic trace.

This is not "search for factors." It is reading the deterministic skeleton that products of two large primes expose because the primes themselves are zero-excess locations under the Divisor Normalization Identity and the interiors between them obey the proved GWR maximizer.

### Why This Is the Single Most Significant Upgrade

1. **It is the highest-visibility, highest-stakes application the program has engaged.** Current results are suggestive on 40-bit and 64-bit static rungs with some public resolutions (e.g., (1048559, 1048589) and (3221225473, 3221275501) classes) and one honest 50-bit unresolved case. A reliable, public, deterministic procedure at 256–1024-bit scales that emits checkable endpoint classes or clean unresolved states would be impossible to dismiss. It demonstrates that the arithmetic foundation proved in PROOF.md has consequences for the multiplicative structure of integers at the exact scales where the rest of the world cares.

2. **It is the strongest forcing function across the rest of the program (multi-front amplification).**
   - Large semiprimes contain many internal prime gaps, including high-utilization square branches near the roots. The endpoint resolver will automatically generate the exact large-gap populations needed to pressure and potentially close the square-branch dynamic cutoff (the current ACTIVE_TARGET).
   - Every step requires explicit naming and transport of chamber-reset signatures, carriers, and lock state. This directly elevates the "unnamed implementation details" identified in FRAME_GOVERNANCE_REVIEW.md and START_HERE.md (chain-horizon, endpoint determinacy, chamber reset) to first-class, testable objects.
   - Structural certificates become first-class portable artifacts. This is exactly the material the Lean formalization track needs beyond the core next-prime/GWR theorems (see Formal Methods Specialist and Theorem Guardian proposals).
   - State-budget questions become crisp: does the same low-dimensional carrier set (d4_count, carrier offset/d pair, previous-gap match, linear B(I) drift control) that works on small chambers and 10^18 surfaces continue to suffice when the interval is defined by a cryptographic modulus? The State Budget Analyst explicitly called the RSA resolver the highest-leverage path to scale-independent bounds on hidden state.
   - Gap-type / Semiprime Wheel Attractor work receives generated (not merely catalogued) type streams inside real semiprime interiors at scale.

3. **It is the point of maximum temptation to violate the deterministic contract — and therefore the best place to demonstrate that the contract holds.**
   Cryptologic work is where the pressure to "just use a quick gcd or MR to confirm" or "treat PGS as a candidate filter" is strongest. Successfully delivering real, public results here while staying 100% inside PGS inference (GWR carriers, transport, reciprocal conditions; no classical selector inside the rule; audit only after) would be the strongest possible internal and external validation of the AGENTS.md and cryptology contract. The Cryptologic Strategist proposal explicitly replaces ad-hoc numeric carrier-alignment bounds with explicit GWR-derived closure conditions.

4. **It converts measured surfaces into a general, scalable engine and produces citable public artifacts.**
   The 40/64-bit successes and 50-bit unresolved case become calibration and diagnostic examples for a uniform rule. Each public resolution (or honest unresolved) becomes new data and a citable claim. Structural certificates make the claims independently verifiable without re-running the full transport. This directly addresses the Documentation Guardian's concern for clean, drift-resistant public framing and the Classical Comparator's desire for decisive "zero classical inference work inside selection" comparison surfaces.

5. **It is a true single upgrade with compounding returns rather than a narrow sub-project.**
   Once the engine exists and works at 256–512 bits, extension to larger sizes is incremental (more steps in the chain, same rules). The same machinery improves the generator story (better large-gap data), the bounded story (square-branch pressure), the formal story (interesting lemmas worth checking), the state-budget story (real pressure on carrier minimality), and the predictions story (new gap-type and reset data for cross-problem forecasts). Delaying it leaves the cryptologic track as "interesting small-rung demos" while the other frontiers continue to lack the natural large-scale adversarial data that RSA moduli supply.

### Current Blockers It Removes or Converts into Diagnostics
- The 50-bit (and future) unresolved_by_reciprocal_carrier_misalignment cases become inputs to a residual classifier rather than open-ended blockers.
- The perception that "PGS only does small primes or is just a fast prefilter."
- The lack of sustained pressure on chamber reset, endpoint determinacy, and chain-horizon closure at the scales that matter.
- The separation between "ordinary gap" bounded-compression work and "semiprime interior" data.

### Concrete Artifacts the Upgrade Would Produce
- Public endpoint-class resolutions (with certificates) on at least one 256-bit and one 512-bit (or larger) semiprime or historical RSA example.
- A documented, versioned "RSA endpoint resolver v3 algorithm" expressed purely in PGS objects (endpoint chain, GWR-carrier transport closure, floor/reciprocal conditions, residual taxonomy).
- Structural certificate format + standalone verifier (Python + ideally Lean-checkable).
- A corpus of resolved and unresolved RSA-scale endpoint examples with full diagnostic traces, automatically feeding state-budget, gap-type, square-branch, and falsification pipelines.
- Updated measurements (carrier counts, B(I) linearity, d=4 dominance, resolution rates) on the new populations.
- A falsification plan and pre-registered adversarial probe set (per Experimental Auditor) that includes carrier-misalignment constructions and square-branch stress inside the moduli.

### How the Winner Addresses Risks Raised by Other Agents
- Bounded Compression Lead concern (empirical foundation): The resolver generates precisely the square-branch data needed to close or falsify the D(r) obligation at larger scales. Work on the resolver can be paired with the hourly square-branch obligation.
- Theorem Guardian / Formalist concern (prose-only theorems): Certificates and the transport rules become the first interesting objects to formalize beyond the core; the core Lean work remains valuable in parallel or as prerequisite.
- Continuity Keeper concern (drift): The winner proposal itself (GWR-selected carrier transport closure) is explicitly PGS-native. The Continuity persona's veto power and the explicit "unresolved with diagnostics" contract are part of the upgrade definition.
- High-Scale Engineer concern (Python wall): The upgrade requires (and therefore drives) the GMP chamber generalization.

### Why the Strongest Runners-Up Ranked Lower
- **Lean formalization of core + certificates:** Extremely high long-term value and epistemic status change. However, it is most powerful once there is a richer body of complex resolved cases (transport rules, carrier closure conditions, large-gap populations) worth formalizing. Formalizing only the existing prose + finite base without the new pressure from cryptologic work was judged narrower.
- **Square-branch proof / proximity closure:** This is the acknowledged central obligation and must be pursued. But it is narrower in scope than the RSA resolver, which attacks the obligation while also moving the highest-visibility application surface and generating the data for the proof. The resolver and square-branch work are synergistic, not mutually exclusive; the counsel judged the resolver the larger single lever.
- **High-scale C productionization alone:** Necessary infrastructure. Without the compelling "what for" supplied by general RSA endpoint resolution (and the resulting demand for 1024-bit+ chamber + transport), it risks becoming engineering for its own sake rather than new mathematical pressure.
- **Frontier Falsification Engine:** Essential hygiene and self-correction. It should be institutionalized as part of executing the winner (and every other frontier), but it is an operating mode and protection layer rather than the primary substantive upgrade.
- **Elevate chamber/chain objects to first-class (Continuity Keeper primary):** This is prerequisite work that the RSA resolver will force. The counsel accepted the Continuity Keeper's runner-up placement of the full resolver and the veto on doing the resolver *before* naming the objects; the winner is understood to include or immediately trigger this elevation.
- **Gap-type generative engine and predictions leverage:** High long-term moonshot potential. These become much more powerful once the resolver supplies generated type streams inside real large semiprime interiors.

### Alignment With PGS-First, Deterministic Contract, and Theorem Trust
The winner keeps the first frame as "locked PGS endpoint chain" (not "factor N"). Every inference step is justified from proved objects (GWR maximizer from PROOF.md) or explicitly measured carrier/transport rules stated with hypotheses and regimes. New "laws" (e.g., generalized GWR-carrier reciprocal closure) will be documented exactly like the current RSA v2 law: public, with exact measured status, and with unresolved states emitted cleanly. PROOF.md theorems remain untouched and upstream. Audit (classical factor confirmation) happens after the PGS inference and does not choose the endpoint class. The generator contract (minimal p/q or endpoint-class record + sidecar diagnostics) is preserved.

This upgrade does not claim to factor RSA or break cryptography. It claims to read and certify deterministic structure that RSA moduli expose when the primes are treated as zero-excess locations under the Divisor Normalization Identity and the interiors obey the proved GWR rule. Whether that structure ultimately has cryptanalytic consequences is a separate, downstream question. The immediate significance for the PGS research program is that it would be the single clearest, highest-leverage demonstration that the local arithmetic foundation has deep, previously under-explored consequences at the scales and in the multiplicative settings that matter most to the broader world — while generating exactly the pressure and data needed to advance every other active frontier.
