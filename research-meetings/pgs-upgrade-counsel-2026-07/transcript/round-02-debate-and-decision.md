# Round 02 to 04: Debate, Convergence, and Decision

**Facilitator synthesis from the 12 agent proposals (see round-01-consolidated-proposals.md for raw inputs).**

## Debate Dimensions Applied

- Theorem leverage vs. blocker removal (square-branch D(r) vs. RSA 50-bit unresolved vs. unnamed chamber objects).
- Multi-front amplification.
- PGS purity (no classical first frame; state separation; generator contract).
- External impact and citable artifacts.
- Feasibility without miracle proofs.
- Risk of status quo (continued siloing, perception of "toy scale only", prose-only theorems).

## Major Convergences

- v1.1 generator is a completed milestone; "more audit tables on small ranges" is not a significant upgrade.
- Any winner must produce new artifact classes (certificates, public large-scale endpoint classes or honest unresolveds, machine-checkable lemmas, or decisive falsification catalogs).
- Three strong families: RSA endpoint resolver maturation, Lean formalization of core + transport, square-branch closure/proof.
- Cryptologic work at real bit lengths is the only proposal that simultaneously (a) moves the highest-visibility application, (b) generates the large natural gaps needed for square-branch pressure, and (c) forces naming of chamber/chain objects.

## Points of Dissent / Pressure

- Bounded Compression Lead: "Any large-scale claim while square-branch remains open builds on an empirical foundation."
- Theorem Guardian / Formal Methods: "Without machine-checked core, outsiders will continue to read everything as empirical."
- Cryptologic + High-Scale + State Budget: "Only public, checkable results on cryptographic moduli will generate the sustained external pressure and the exact adversarial data the other frontiers need."
- Continuity & Shape Keeper: Vetoed high-scale C or broad predictions as flagship before chain-horizon / chamber / endpoint-determinacy objects are first-class and named. Accepted the RSA resolver as runner-up in their own proposal when properly framed as PGS-native.

## Decision Rule (applied)

The upgrade that most powerfully amplifies the deterministic core across the largest number of active frontiers, directly attacks a visible high-stakes unresolved surface while generating data for the current central obligation, produces new public auditable artifacts (classical audit post-facto only), and can be executed while rigorously maintaining PGS-native inference.

## Winner

Maturation and generalization of the deterministic PGS-native RSA modulus endpoint resolver (explicit GWR-selected carrier transport closure under reciprocal floor maps on chamber-reset certificates + structural certificates) to cryptographically relevant scales.

**Rationale summary (drawn from agent outputs):**
- Directly attacks the 50-bit unresolved and rung-specific limitation.
- Forces chamber reset, endpoint determinacy, and chain-horizon to become named, transportable objects.
- Generates large-gap (including square-branch) data inside real semiprimes.
- Produces structural certificates, the exact artifacts Lean and external verification need.
- Forces state-budget clarity on whether the low-dimensional carrier set remains sufficient at scale.
- Highest external perception impact while staying inside the strict PGS contract (GWR carriers, not gcd or MR inside inference).
- Synergistic with, rather than replacement for, square-branch and Lean work.
