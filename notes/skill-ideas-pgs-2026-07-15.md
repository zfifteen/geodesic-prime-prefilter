**Repo:** `~/IdeaProjects/prime-gap-structure`

## Build first (highest leverage)

### 1. `pgs-native-frame`
Lock every turn to objects → invariants → rules → resolved/unresolved/invalidated.  
Block classical primality/factor gates as inference.  
Reject probabilistic reframing of proved local laws.  
Emit a short frame checklist before heavy reasoning.

### 2. `pgs-claim-status`
Classify any claim as: **theorem** (in PROOF.md) | **measured** | **audit** | **hypothesis** | **unresolved**.  
Refuse upgraded language.  
For program-level verified/validated wording, require the **10^18 evidence surface** or force weaker labels.

### 3. `pgs-gwr-dni-check`
Specialist check for GWR / DNI / leftmost min-d(n) / Z-E coordinate misuse.  
Verify selection of interior witness, bounded compression statements, and dual Z = e^{-E} consistency in prose and code.

### 4. `pgs-falsify-one`
Extract **one** PGS claim → design/run the smallest falsifier under `experiments/` → report regime and outcome.  
Default labels: local probe vs program-level.

### 5. `pgs-qa-gate`
Run the universal closing gate from AGENTS.md explicitly: review plan → execute → fix → pass/fail table.  
Mandatory before any "done" on code, proof, experiment, or RC answer.

### 6. `pgs-hourly-relay`
Operate within HOURLY_RELAY_CONTRACT: worktree, `/heavy` default, square-branch continuity, RC notify non-fatal.  
Detect drift from prior hour; produce next minimal advance only.

### 7. `pgs-rsa-engine-audit`
Audit RSA / cryptology path under `research/06-cryptology-rsa/` against AGENTS cryptology contract:  
locked endpoint chains, floor transport, reciprocal closure, residual class, certificate vs unresolved.  
No classical factoring as PGS inference.

### 8. `pgs-lean-status`
Map Lean-4 formalization surface (`lean-4/`) to PROOF.md claims: what is formalized, what is gap, next lemma to formalize.

### 9. `pgs-rh-separation`
Guard `docs/rh/` and any RH-adjacent prose: keep **reading path / compression story** strictly separated from proved local core.  
Prevent "RH follows from GWR" style leaks.

### 10. `pgs-sibling-bridge`
Bridge work across `pgs_lab`, `pgs-java`, `pgs-games`, `prime-gap-structure-extensions` without forking the generator contract.  
Name which repo owns the artifact.
