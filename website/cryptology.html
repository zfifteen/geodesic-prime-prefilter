05 · Cryptology

Factorization-adjacent research, without the classical script

This chapter is a full public technical brief on the live cryptology front of the program. The objects are endpoint chains, floor transport, reciprocal closure, residual state, and structural certificates. The frame is not ordinary search over candidate factors.

Status discipline

Nothing on this page claims that RSA is broken at scale. Toy and mid-bit ladder rows are measured inside exact case IDs. Open cases remain unresolved. Universal factorization theorems are not smuggled in through educational prose.

The required research frame

Ordinary factoring stories often begin with candidate factors, divisibility checks, product closure, or probabilistic primality gates. That is not the first frame here.

The PGS cryptologic path starts from locked structure on a public modulus and asks whether transport and reciprocal closure finish as a certificate. If the invariants do not finish, the answer is unresolved. 

locked endpoint chain
  → floor transport through the modulus
  → reciprocal endpoint closure
  → modulus-link residual
  → structural certificate  or  unresolved state

What “endpoint” means here

In the generator chapters, endpoints are the primes that wall a gap. In the cryptology front, endpoint language lifts to public structural positions associated with a modulus: ordered places the program can traverse, transport, and attempt to close.

The educational point is the same as in the gap chapters: start with the ordered object, not with a bag of secret guesses.

Reciprocal transport and closure

Floor transport moves information through the public modulus using maps that stay inside integer structure. Reciprocal closure asks whether the transported chain is consistent when read against its dual orientation.

When closure succeeds on a measured case, the program can emit a public endpoint class: a resolved structural pair associated with the modulus under the active rule set. When closure fails the consistency checks, the row stays unresolved.

The live RSA v2 research law in the repository is described as reciprocal deadline-signature correction plus oriented endpoint-chain closure: a public PGS endpoint-class resolver.

That is a research and measurement contract on named cases. It is not a claim that every RSA modulus of cryptographic size has been resolved by the same public path.

Audit may later confirm exact factor pairs on resolved toy cases. Audit does not define the inference rule, and audit success on a small case does not promote the rule to a universal RSA-scale theorem.

What we refuse to do in public language

  • We do not rephrase this work as “basically ordinary factorization.”
  • We do not treat a survivor count as a factor.
  • We do not treat an audit confirmation as the inference engine.
  • We do not upgrade a toy ladder into an RSA-scale theorem.
  • We do not hide unresolved rows behind success theater.
Why the refusal matters

Cryptology is where educational sites most often inflate claims. Prime Gap Structure keeps the same honesty rules on this page that it uses on the theorem page: separate proved, measured, audit, hypothesis, and unresolved.
