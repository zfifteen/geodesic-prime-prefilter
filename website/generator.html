04 · Generator

Known prime in.
Next prime out.

The Minimal PGS Generator turns the gap story into a production contract: given a known prime, emit the successor prime as a minimal record. Diagnostics stay outside the stream.

The output is deliberately small

For each resolved input prime, the generator emits exactly a pair: 

{"p": 89, "q": 97}

That record says only what matters: start at 89; the next prime is 97. No confidence fields. No source labels. No audit metadata inside the stream.

Certificates and diagnostics can exist as sidecars. The generated answer itself stays clean.

A different question than “is this prime?”

Conventional generators often propose a candidate and test it. If the candidate fails, they try another. Faster tests improve the loop, but the shape remains propose and test.

The PGS generator asks where the interval after a known prime closes. It reads structural state in the chamber after p and returns q under the program’s deterministic selection contract.

Contract

input known prime p → output next prime q

If the selection rule does not resolve, the honest result is an explicit unresolved state. The contract does not authorize a silent classical fallback search to invent an answer.

Audit after generation

Audit role

Classical checks may verify an emitted pair after the fact. Audit confirms. Audit does not choose q. That separation is part of the public engineering posture of the project.

Implementation evidence (not theorem bounds)

Measured

Production surfaces record exact outputs with zero unresolved rows and zero audit failures inside stated regimes. Those tables certify the implementation. They do not replace the universal next-prime theorem in PROOF.md.

The generator contract is p → q from exact divisor-count structure. Universal bounded compression (proved) places the GWR-selected witness within max(64, ceil(0.5 · (log q)²)) of the left endpoint. Validation surfaces confirm implementation fidelity inside their regimes; they are not the theorem boundary.

The live production iteration is the PGS-only v1.1 generator path documented in the repository release notes. It is a completed major production milestone. It is not the whole active research program.
