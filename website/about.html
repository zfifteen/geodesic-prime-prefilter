Program status map

What is proved, what is implemented, what is measured, and what remains open. This page is a public continuity surface, not a biography.

Proved

Universal local pillars

Direct next-prime rule from exact divisor counts. Interior maximizer (leftmost minimum-divisor selection). Universal bounded compression of the selected-witness offset, including the Prime-Square Proximity Theorem.

Authority: PROOF.md

Implemented

Minimal PGS Generator v1.1

Production milestone: known prime in, next prime out as a minimal record. Completed major release, not the whole active project.

Map: Generator chapter · generator doc

Measured

Generator and walk surfaces

Including 11 .. 1,000,000 with full exact output and zero unresolved rows, decade-window surfaces through 10^18, and recursive walk recoveries inside stated regimes.

Map: Evidence chapter · RESULTS.md

Active research

Cryptology and modulus-link work

Endpoint-chain traversal, floor transport, reciprocal closure, modulus-link residual state, and structural certificates on public ladders. Includes resolved and unresolved RSA v2 rows.

Map: Cryptology chapter · research tree

Open / careful

What this program does not claim here

PROOF.md does not itself prove the Riemann Hypothesis. Bounded compression bounds the selected-witness offset; it does not automatically prove every classical gap-size conjecture. Cryptology ladders are not a universal RSA-scale theorem.

Lean 4 formalization of the local pillars is in progress as a machine-checked mirror.

What this website is

A public educational course for curious non-specialists: dark-luxury editorial design, dual reading depth, interactive visual labs, and strict status labels.

It is not the formal proof. It is not a substitute for repository artifacts. When teaching language and formal status conflict, formal status wins.
