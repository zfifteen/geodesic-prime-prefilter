A public course in structure, not chance

The integers after a prime
are not noise.

Prime Gap Structure is a research program about what sits between consecutive primes: the ordered chamber of composites, the way divisor counts mark that chamber, and the deterministic laws that name the next prime.

Begin the course What is proved
Proved

Next prime from structure

Given a known prime, exact divisor counts on the integers after it determine the next prime. This is a universal theorem under its stated hypotheses.

Proved

A distinguished composite

Inside a nonempty gap, one interior integer is selected by a simple left-to-right minimum-divisor rule. It is the unique maximizer of a fixed comparison score.

Proved

Bounded compression

That selected witness cannot sit arbitrarily far from the left prime. Its offset is bounded at a precise logarithmic scale. This bounds the witness, not every classical gap-size conjecture.
