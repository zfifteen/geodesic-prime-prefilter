06 · Evidence

Measured surfaces, exact regimes

This page is a public map of implementation and experiment evidence. Every row is a tested regime. None of these tables is a substitute for the universal theorems in PROOF.md.

How to read this page

Proved statements live on the Laws page and in PROOF.md. Measured rows below certify code and probes inside the stated range. Mixing those columns is how educational sites accidentally downgrade theorems or inflate benchmarks.

Recursive walk surfaces

Measured

The exact recursive walk repeats the direct next-prime step from prime to prime. On the verified surfaces recorded in RESULTS.md, consecutive recovery stays exact inside the stated ranges, including a long walk from prime 11 through prime 10,000,121 with zero skipped gaps on that surface.

A sampled decade ladder from 10^2 through 10^18 also stayed at exact hit rate 1.0 with zero skipped gaps across 860 measured recursive steps.

What evidence is not

  • Evidence is not a license to call a theorem “empirical.”
  • Evidence is not a factorization of cryptographic RSA moduli at scale.
  • Evidence is not permission to drop unresolved rows from the public story.

Primary maps: docs/RESULTS.md, generator documentation under docs/, and cryptology artifacts under research/06-cryptology-rsa/. Theorem statements and proof text: PROOF.md.

If a number on this educational site ever disagrees with the repository artifacts, the repository artifacts win until the site is corrected.
