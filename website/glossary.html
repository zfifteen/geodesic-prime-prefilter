07 · Glossary

Names after objects

Project vocabulary is useful once you can picture the thing being named. Each entry starts with the ordinary object, then the term.

Prime gap
The open stretch between two consecutive primes: all composites strictly between them, together with the two prime walls.
Chamber
A teaching word for the interior region after a known prime and before the next prime. Emphasizes that the composites form a readable room, not random debris.
Divisor count · τ(n)
How many positive divisors an integer has. Primes greater than 1 have divisor count 2.
Next-prime rule (direct)
After a known prime, the first later integer with divisor count 2 is the next prime. Proved
Selected witness · w
Inside a nonempty gap, the first interior integer that achieves the minimum divisor count.
Leftmost Minimum-Divisor Rule · GWR
The project name for the selection that produces the selected witness. Proved to be the unique maximizer of the comparison score F. Proved
Comparison score · F(n)
A fixed function of divisor count and size. On a gap interior, F is maximized uniquely at the selected witness.
Zero excess · E(n)
A coordinate that vanishes exactly on primes (for n > 1). Related to F by F = −E in the proof’s dual reading.
Divisor Normalization Identity · DNI
The preferred exact coordinate reformulation around zero excess and its dual multiplicative form. An identity/coordinate system, not a new theorem by itself.
Bounded compression
The proved statement that the selected-witness offset w − p cannot exceed a dynamic cutoff at logarithmic-square scale. Proved
Prime-Square Proximity Theorem
The proved square-branch closure used inside universal bounded compression. Proved
Minimal PGS Generator
Production contract: known prime in, next prime out, as a minimal JSON pair. Diagnostics stay outside the stream.
Unresolved state
An explicit non-answer when the active PGS rule does not close. Not a cue to invent a classical fallback as if it were PGS inference.
Endpoint chain
An ordered sequence of structural endpoints the program traverses. In cryptology work, the chain is the public working object for transport and closure.
Floor transport
Moving structural information through a modulus using floor-scale maps, rather than candidate-factor guessing.
Reciprocal closure
Consistency of transported endpoint structure under reciprocal reading. Success can yield a public endpoint class; failure yields unresolved.
Modulus-link residual
What remains after transport and attempted closure when the story is not finished: informative structure without a completed certificate.
Structural certificate
A finished public structural emission under the active rule (for example a resolved endpoint class on a measured case).
Audit
Downstream verification of an already produced answer. May use classical tools. Does not choose the PGS output.
Measured surface
An implementation or experiment result with an exact tested regime. Certifies code and probes; does not bound universal theorems by itself.
