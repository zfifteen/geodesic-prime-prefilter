## Part 1: Core Insight

```insight
Breakthrough Residual Reclassification

Closing the local PGS breakthrough stack does not make every remaining problem a little easier. It reclassifies what kind of residual can still exist.

After the consecutive-gap pillars close (next-prime rule, GWR maximizer, universal witness compression, square-branch proximity, and twin resonance as a special lock), failures are no longer free to be "the bound was too weak" or "the square branch leaked." Those residual classes are gone.

What remains open, if anything, is transfer residual: public containment of a neighborhood, exact identity recovery inside that neighborhood, and residual-zero alignment on endpoint chains.

That is non-obvious because the program looks like a pile of stronger theorems. The pile is real, but its joint effect is class elimination, not uniform hardness reduction.

What you would not have predicted without this view is a mature post-breakthrough surface where routing containment is already perfect while exact recovery still fails, and every failure still sits inside the routed window.

That is exactly the readable signature on the committed 127-bit official gate: twelve of twelve factors sit in the final window, nine of twelve recover exactly, and all three misses remain in-window, concentrated off the balanced close-pair family.

The principle is expected to hold on measured PGS crypto and endpoint surfaces after the 2026-07 pillar stack is in force. It is likely to break if a surface still has open local residual classes (pre-square-branch, fixed-cutoff thinking, or unproved witness placement), or if a harness reports high containment while factors are actually outside windows.

The mechanism makes the pattern robust: pillars kill local residual branch classes of consecutive-gap witness placement. They do not by themselves supply the operator that turns a correctly located public neighborhood into a recovered factor endpoint or a residual-zero modulus link. So containment can saturate first, and identity or residual-zero can lag without contradicting the proved stack.
```
