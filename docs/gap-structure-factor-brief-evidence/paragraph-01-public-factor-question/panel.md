# Panel 01: The Public Factor Question

## Final Paragraph

Factoring starts with one visible number and two hidden prime factors. This
research branch asks whether the visible order around that number contains
enough structure to point back to one of the hidden factors. The work treats
the product as sitting inside a public neighborhood whose ordered marks can be
carried from one side of the product to the other. The central question is
whether that public structure forces a factor endpoint, or whether the honest
result remains unresolved.
