# Panel 08: Invalidated Shortcut

## Final Paragraph

The branch also ruled out a tempting shortcut. A broad shadow pattern appeared
in the measured surface, and at first it looked like a candidate for separating
the wrong lanes from the surviving lanes. The wider check showed the same
shadow on the survivor lanes as well. That makes the shadow real, but not
selector-grade. The result prevents the project from promoting a local pattern
into a proof step that the evidence does not support.
