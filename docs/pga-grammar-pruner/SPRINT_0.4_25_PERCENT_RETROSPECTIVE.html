Rule Expansion History (How 5 → 37 Rules Produced the 25%+ Result)

Phase 0 (seed): PG-001 to 005 from the original 601_5500 factor-class exclusion pivot. Delivered the initial ~10% on a2@mid and a4@mid motifs.

Phase 1: PG-006 to 012 added phase asymmetry, prev-bias, and o6-family rules (still mostly 601_5500 + light cross-band). Pushed some cases into the mid-teens.

Phase 2 (the crossing): PG-013 to 037 mined from the 27001_30000, 32001_34000, and 34001_35000 directional boundary gate surfaces and their enriched multiplication map corpora. These surfaces exposed the very_late fringe, early-heavy, and specific L/R reversal patterns under a2/a4/a6 contexts that were invisible or weak on the smaller 601_5500 band. Each new tranche of 4 rules per major motif family added ~4 to 6% more pruning power on the dominant o2_a2@mid cases, taking them from ~10% to 28.79%.

The final nine-rule union for the eight dominant toy N values (PG-001+002+008+010+018+022+026+030+034) is what produced the 57-count that pushed the average over the line.
