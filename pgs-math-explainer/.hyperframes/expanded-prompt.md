## Rhythm

Pattern: hook-hold-build-build-SLOW-build-PEAK-breathe-proof-proof-proof-resolve.

The first beat is direct and contract-led. The middle beats layer arithmetic state into the frame. The peak is the endpoint rule, where selected-integer structure and candidate reset become a single output. The final third is empirical surface, proof boundary, and invariant summary.

## Global Rules

- 300 seconds total, 1920 by 1080, twelve 25-second scenes.
- Every scene has a background texture, mathematical midground, and foreground labels.
- Transitions use gentle blur crossfades by overlapping adjacent scenes for 0.8 seconds.
- Entrances are explicit for each scene element.
- The final scene alone fades to black.
- No random placement, no runtime-generated variation, no infinite repeats.

## Scene Beats

1. Contract. The viewer sees the generator's whole job before any formalism: input prime `p`, output next prime `q`, record contains exactly two keys.
2. Search interval. The region right of `p` becomes a finite arithmetic chamber, not a primality-oracle sweep.
3. Wheel-open offsets. Candidate boundary positions appear as mod 30 residues, filtering where endpoint hypotheses can live.
4. Divisor count state. Each integer in the chamber carries exact divisor-count load.
5. DNI. The fixed-point idea is introduced from observed count and size: primes sit at `Z(n)=1`; composites fall below.
6. GWR integer. The leftmost minimum-divisor integer is selected as a landmark, not as the endpoint.
7. Endpoint Rule X. Candidate hypotheses, composite rejection, integer lock, lower-divisor threat ceiling, and chamber reset produce `q`.
8. No fallback. The generator either resolves by PGS or raises `PGSUnresolvedError`; it does not call trial division, Miller-Rabin, `isprime`, `nextprime`, or a sieve.
9. Minimal output, sidecar diagnostics. The output stream stays physically minimal; source labels and diagnostics live beside it.
10. Audited surfaces. Low-scale and high-scale tested regimes appear as measured output shares and audit failures.
11. Proof pressure. Empirical compression is separated from theorem. The fixed cutoff theorem is already false at `q = 24098209`; current compression is empirical.
12. Closing invariant. PGS is framed as state divided by invariant limit and rate divided by the same limit: the ratio-first discipline behind the generator.

## Recurring Motifs

## Negative Prompt

No generic prime-number folklore. No randomness. No fallback search diagrams. No labels implying audit chooses `q`. No treating the selected integer as the endpoint. No unsupported theorem claim.
