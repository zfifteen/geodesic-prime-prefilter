PGS Program Enhancement Roadmap

Formal catalog of possible enhancements to the Prime Gap Structure program. Lead with the concrete object under pressure, then the mechanism, then the project label, then status. This surface does not promote measured results to theorems and does not claim RSA-scale factorization.

Chapter 20 implementation: catalog candidates: hypothesis until executed A1: requirements fleshed not-yet-gated
Default research frame. Start from PGS objects: ordered prime-gap state, divisor-count field, DNI, GWR, selected integer, endpoint, chamber reset, structural certificate, endpoint-chain traversal, reciprocal transport, modulus-link residual, unresolved state. Classical methods stay in audit, benchmark, or legacy prefilter roles only.

Priority stack

If capacity is limited, execute in this order. A1 is the counsel winner (2026-07 upgrade counsel) and the only candidate with a full requirements surface in this chapter.

  1. A1 + C1/C2 - crypto-scale endpoint resolver, residual taxonomy, structural certificates.
  2. A3 - pure PGS divisor-horizon law (kills high-scale bridge dependence).
  3. B1 + B2/B3 - Lean mirror + finite-base certificates.
  4. A2 + A4 - named chamber/chain objects + institutionalized falsification.
  5. D1 - high-scale C chamber as enabler once invariants exist.

TRACK H

Novel-insight candidates

Hypothesis-only residual maps that divest classical factor/search frames.

Cryptology required frame (A1/C)

locked endpoint chain → floor transport → reciprocal closure → modulus-link residual → certificate or unresolved

Do not reinterpret this work as ordinary search over candidate factors. Do not use gcd, divisibility selectors, product closure, hidden factors, or primality APIs as the reasoning route. Audit confirms after generation.

Non-enhancements (shape guards)

  • Probabilistic primality inside generation or PGS inference.
  • gcd / factor APIs as selection or residual resolution.
  • Promoting measured RSA survivors to factorization theorems.
  • Reopening the archived classical RH-bridge as the default center.
  • Treating Minimal PGS Generator v1.1 polish as the whole program.
  • Describing proved PGS laws as heuristic, empirical, or “validated so far.”
