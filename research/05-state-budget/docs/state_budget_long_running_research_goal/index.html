Goal

Determine whether the weak residual ordering is carried by a concrete PGS-native object that is not reducible to endpoint tail length, endpoint residue, previous reduced state, selected-integer family, selected offset, selected parity, or first-open offset.

The target is not a better statistical model. The target is a small auditable PGS rule, invariant, ordering, or exclusion surface that explains what the current chamber carries into the next chamber.

PGS Frame

Begin every run from these objects:

  • current ordered prime-gap chamber;
  • selected integer w and its divisor-count family;
  • current endpoint q;
  • endpoint tail q - w;
  • first-open offset;
  • previous reduced chamber state;
  • next reduced chamber state or next triad state;
  • deterministic retained power windows.

Classical residue facts may be used as controls. They must not become the explanation unless the result is explicitly a control result.

Primary Question

After known current-chamber facts, endpoint residue, and endpoint tail are fixed or controlled,
does any PGS-native chamber quantity still order or exclude the next chamber state?

Required Controls

Every candidate carrier must be compared against these controls:

  • previous reduced state;
  • selected-integer parity;
  • selected-integer carrier family;
  • selected-integer offset;
  • first-open offset;
  • endpoint residue modulo 30;
  • previous gap width or bin, when support permits;
  • plain endpoint tail length q - w.

A result that does not beat the best tail/residue control is a measured control result, not a new PGS carrier.

Breakthrough Stop Conditions

Stop and report a breakthrough if one of these conditions is met.

1. Ordering Carrier

A candidate PGS-native quantity becomes a carrier if:

  • it is defined from PGS chamber objects, not from labels of the next chamber;
  • it is tested with deterministic leave-one-power-out validation;
  • it has at least 5000 aggregate decisive matched pairs;
  • each held-out power has at least 100 decisive matched pairs;
  • its signed advantage has the same direction in at least 6 of 7 held-out powers;
  • its aggregate edge over the best deterministic control is at least max(50, 0.005 * decisive_pairs) signed wins;
  • no simpler deterministic control reaches that margin.

2. Exclusion Carrier

A candidate PGS-native quantity becomes an exclusion carrier if:

  • it removes at least one possible next state inside matched current-chamber cells;
  • it has at least 1000 eligible held-out rows;
  • each held-out power has at least 100 eligible rows;
  • aggregate held-out violation rate is at most 0.01;
  • no held-out power has violation rate above 0.02;
  • the same exclusion rule works after adding endpoint residue and tail controls, unless the rule explicitly proves why those controls are the carrier.

3. Proof Carrier

A symbolic PGS derivation is a breakthrough if it derives a current-to-next chamber constraint from existing PGS objects and states exact hypotheses. The proof must separate theorem status from measured confirmation.

Blocker Stop Conditions

Stop and report an insurmountable blocker if one of these conditions is met.

  • The deterministic retained surface is expanded to at least 8192 consecutive rows per power for 10^12..10^18, and no candidate PGS-native quantity beats endpoint tail plus residue by the ordering-carrier margin.
  • Candidate exclusions repeatedly shrink menus but violate above 0.05 after endpoint tail and residue controls on a surface with at least 5000 eligible held-out rows.
  • Exact-tail or stricter matched cells remain below 1000 eligible held-out rows after the 8192-row-per-power surface, making the residual question unsupported by the current catalog design.
  • Catalog construction cannot reach the required deterministic surface because the current certificate builder is too slow or memory-bound. Report the largest completed surface, runtime, row count, and the missing support needed.
  • The research path requires randomness, trained black-box models, fallback search, primality APIs as inference, or next-state label leakage. Stop and report shape failure instead of continuing.

Do Not Pursue

  • Do not re-promote the square-ruler interpretation without beating tail length.
  • Do not run random splits, shuffled randomness, or stochastic searches.
  • Do not optimize for a generic prediction model.
  • Do not use future or next-chamber labels to define a current-chamber carrier.
  • Do not widen the branch into general prime-gap memory or classical mutual information.
  • Do not call an unresolved result a weak positive result.
