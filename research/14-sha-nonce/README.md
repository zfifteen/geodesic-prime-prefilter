# SHA Nonce Research

## Object

SHA nonce research studies deterministic nonce-line geometry, reset-centered
argmin position, reduced-round block ranking, and related SHA-256 probe
surfaces.

## Contents

- `docs/`: finding notes and status summaries.
- `scripts/`: deterministic SHA nonce probes.
- `tests/`: chapter-local tests for nonce probes.
- `output/`: committed JSON and SVG measurement artifacts.

## Status

The chapter preserves measured SHA nonce evidence separately from PGS theorem
status. It is not a PGS proof surface.
