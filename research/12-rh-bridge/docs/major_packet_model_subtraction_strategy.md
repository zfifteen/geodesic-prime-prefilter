# Major Packet Model Subtraction Strategy

Date: 2026-05-24

Status: construction strategy and compatibility check for major packet
subtraction before the direct large-sieve estimate.

The direct continuous-frequency large sieve estimates an exponential sum

$$
A^{\perp}(\alpha)=\sum_n a_n^{\perp}e(\alpha n)
$$

from a coefficient-level residual sequence `a_n^perp`. Therefore major packet
subtraction must produce an actual residual coefficient sequence, not only a
local Fourier approximation on each major window.

## Local Major Packet Model

Near a rational center

$$
c={a\over q},
$$

the standard major packet has the form

$$
B_X(c+\beta)
=
\operatorname{Major}_{c}(\beta)
+
\operatorname{Err}_{c}(\beta).
$$

For a von Mangoldt-weighted model,

$$
\operatorname{Major}_{a/q}(\beta)
\approx
{\mu(q)\over\varphi(q)}
\sum_{X<n\le2X}e(\beta n),
$$

with modifications for endpoint normalization, centering, and exceptional
characters. The centered constant term is explicit and must be included in
the packet model.

## Compatibility Problem

Local subtraction

$$
B_X(a/q+\beta)-\operatorname{Major}_{a/q}(\beta)
$$

does not automatically equal

$$
\sum_n a_n^{\perp}e((a/q+\beta)n)
$$

for one global residual sequence `a_n^perp` independent of `a/q`.

The direct additive large-sieve route needs such a global residual. Otherwise
the proof must remain in the character-decomposed framework, where the
residual is a family of nonprincipal character sums.

## Coefficient-Level Projection Route

Define a finite-dimensional major subspace spanned by low-conductor coherent
sequences:

$$
\mathcal V_{\mathrm{maj}}
=
\operatorname{span}\{
1,\ e(an/q),\ \chi(n),\ \text{exceptional coherent components}
:\ q\le Q_0
\}.
$$

Let

$$
P_{\mathrm{maj}}
$$

be a bounded projection onto this subspace in the coefficient Hilbert space.
Then set

$$
a^{\perp}=a-P_{\mathrm{maj}}a.
$$

Now

$$
A^{\perp}(\alpha)=\sum_n a_n^{\perp}e(\alpha n)
$$

is a genuine coefficient-level residual, and the direct large sieve applies.

## Character-Level Route

If a bounded coefficient projection is not constructed, use character
decomposition instead:

$$
\operatorname{Err}_{a/q}(\beta)
=
{1\over\varphi(q)}
\sum_{\chi\bmod q}^{\mathrm{nonmajor}}
\overline{\chi(a)}
S_{\chi}(\beta)
+
\text{controlled imprimitive terms}.
$$

Then the proof uses the weighted hybrid character/additive large sieve, not
the direct additive large sieve for a single residual sequence.

## Required Inputs

The direct route needs:

1. an explicit major subspace `V_maj`;
2. a bounded projection `P_maj`;
3. proof that local major packet models are reproduced by
   `P_maj a` on `|beta| <= R_all`;
4. residual energy bound
   $$
   \mathcal A_2=\|a^{\perp}\|_2^2;
   $$
5. treatment of exceptional coherent terms inside `V_maj`.

## Minimal Lemma

> **Coefficient-Level Major Packet Projection Lemma.**  
> The low-conductor major packet models at radius `R_all` are generated by a
> bounded coefficient-level projection `P_maj`, and the residual
> `a^perp=a-P_maj a` satisfies the energy bound required by the direct
> continuous-frequency large sieve.

## Result

The direct additive large-sieve route is valid only after coefficient-level
major packet projection is constructed. If the major packets remain purely
local Fourier approximations, the proof must use the weighted hybrid
character large-sieve route instead.
