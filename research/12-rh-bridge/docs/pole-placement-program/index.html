DNI/PGS Pole-Placement Program

A research program for proving the RH shadow from the divisor-count object that casts it.

PGS explains the RH shadow. RH does not explain PGS. The prime sequence is fixed by integer-level divisor-count structure first. The zeta-zero geometry is the analytic encoding of that structure after it has already appeared inside the integers.

Core Claim

The upstream object is the ordered arithmetic structure of the integers: divisor counts, chamber interiors, selected integers, and first returns to tau(n)=2.

The downstream object is the meromorphic continuation of the DNI-built ratio whose poles record the zero geometry of zeta(s).

Theorem Frontier

Prove that every nontrivial pole of the continued DNI ratio R(s) lies on Re(s)=1/2.

Classical RH then follows as a corollary because nontrivial poles of R(s) are exactly the nontrivial zeros of zeta(s), with multiplicity encoded by residue.

Completed Ratio Work

Completion Target

Use the completed zeta function to isolate the nontrivial pole ledger from the known pole at s=1 and the trivial-zero gamma structure.

xi(s) = (1/2) s(s-1) pi^(-s/2) Gamma(s/2) zeta(s)

DNI Expression

Define Q(s)=-xi'(s)/xi(s). Then Q(s) equals the DNI ratio plus explicit elementary correction terms.

Q(s) = R(s) - 1/s - 1/(s-1) + (1/2)log(pi) - (1/2)psi(s/2)

In the centered coordinate u=s-1/2, the functional equation gives the necessary symmetry xi(1/2+u)=xi(1/2-u). The open theorem is stronger: every nontrivial pole must sit on Re(u)=0.

Proof Program

1. Canonical Ratio

Lock the definitions of D(s), K(s), R(s), and Q(s). Record every domain, continuation, pole, residue, and removable singularity explicitly.

2. PGS Mangoldt Ledger

Rewrite Lambda(n) from PGS objects: endpoint returns, prime-power echoes, chamber resets, and endpoint-chain traversal.

3. Explicit Formula Transfer

Express every spectral term in the explicit formula as a downstream contribution of the DNI/PGS ledger, with the completion corrections separated.

4. Off-Line Pole Signature

Translate a hypothetical pole with Re(s) != 1/2 into a concrete imbalance in the chamber ledger.

5. Forbidden Imbalance

Prove the chamber invariants forbid that imbalance. This is the main theorem mechanism, not a numerical confirmation layer.

6. Corollary Extraction

Once the pole-placement theorem is proved, state classical RH as the immediate corollary of the DNI/PGS theorem.
