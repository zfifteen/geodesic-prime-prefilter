2. Generality vs. Family-Specific Tuning

Accepted

Correction: The codebase will not replace the sqrt(N) baseline. Instead, the implementation will explicitly utilize the existing --start-anchor parameter, heavily documenting the provenance of any value passed to it. We will avoid hardcoding "0.399" inside the pure inference logic.
