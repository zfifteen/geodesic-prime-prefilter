# Gap-Ridge Research

## Object

Gap-ridge research studies where the gap-local divisor field places its
dominant low-divisor structure, especially the near-edge ridge and the
leftmost minimum-divisor peak rule.

## Contents

- `docs/`: ridge notes, finding writeups, and supporting reports.
- `scripts/`: deterministic ridge probes and validation runners.
- `tests/`: chapter-local tests for ridge probes.
- `output/`: committed ridge measurements, plots, and validation summaries.

## Status

The chapter preserves measured ridge evidence separately from proved PGS
theorems. Root theorem status remains controlled by `PROOF.md`.
