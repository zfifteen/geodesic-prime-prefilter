# Reviewer Questions

## Theorem Correctness

1. Is the direct next-prime theorem stated and proved correctly?
2. Is the interior maximizer theorem stated and proved correctly?
3. Does the proof of the earlier-integer side of the maximizer theorem close
   every retained case?
4. Are the prime-square case and the bounded-compression prime-square
   proximity question clearly separated?

## Self-Contained Sufficiency

1. Does the draft contain every definition needed to evaluate the proof?
2. Are the finite certificate tables sufficient for the proof role assigned to
   them?
3. Does the reproducibility contract state the computational checks clearly
   enough for independent review?

## Status Boundaries

1. Are the two universal theorems stated with the correct strength?
2. Are finite certificates, measured surfaces, audit results, invalidated
   routes, and unresolved boundaries separated clearly?
3. Does any measured or audit result sound like a theorem when it should not?
4. Does any proved theorem sound weaker than proved?

## Publication Readiness

1. Is the abstract accurate and appropriately framed?
2. Is the title accurate?
3. What is the highest-priority revision before Zenodo submission?
4. Are there any claims that should be removed, strengthened, or moved to an
   appendix?
