The main claims under review are:

1. Given a known prime $p$, exact divisor counts determine the next prime:

   $$
   q=\min\{n>p:\tau(n)=2\}.
   $$

2. Inside every nonempty prime gap, the leftmost interior integer with minimum
   divisor count uniquely maximizes

   $$
   F(n)=\left(1-\frac{\tau(n)}{2}\right)\log n.
   $$

bounded-compression status, invalidated routes, and explicit non-claims from
