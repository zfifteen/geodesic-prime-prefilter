# Measured Claim Scale Gate (HARD): `10^18`

**Status:** program policy (HARD, non-negotiable).  
**Canonical full text:** root `AGENTS.md` section **Mandatory 10^18 Evidence Surface**.  
**Short machine rule:** `.grok/rules/pgs-10e18-evidence-surface.md`.

## One sentence

Program-strength **verified**, **validated**, or program-level measured/audit
pass language is allowed only when the evidence package includes an **executed**
surface at magnitude **`10^18`**.

## What this is not

- Not a bound on theorems in `PROOF.md`.
- Not a license to say proved laws are "only verified at `10^18`."
- Not RH, PNT, or RSA-scale resolution from a high-scale measured pass.
